### Justification

VulnCheck KEV confirms CVE-2026-9082 is actively exploited in-the-wild against Drupal Core, mapping directly to T1190 (Exploit Public-Facing Application). The vulnerability is pre-authentication, meaning no credential compromise is required before exploitation — any internet-reachable Drupal instance is a viable target. The Threat Intel classifies exploit maturity as 'ACTIVELY EXPLOITED (Observed in-the-wild)', making this an immediate operational risk rather than a theoretical one. The lab environment exposes public-facing application services, and while Drupal is not listed as a verified asset, the active exploitation status and zero-authentication requirement justify hunting for anomalous web-tier request patterns consistent with JSON:API abuse that could indicate either a Drupal instance not captured in the asset inventory or lateral pivot from an initial foothold.
